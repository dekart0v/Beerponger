Beerponger

Beer pong scores & ratings

Beerponger keeps score at beer pong. Cups come down as they are sunk, ratings move on their own, and a double-elimination bracket can run the whole night. Everything lives on your phone — there is no account and no internet connection.

Common questions

How do I move my crew to a new phone?

Settings (the gear beside the wordmark) → Backup → save the file, then open it on the new phone and load it. Loading never wipes what is already there: players and matches merge by id, and duplicates are dropped.

Can I share my crew with a friend?

Yes — send them the same backup file. Their app merges yours into theirs, including any cards you wrote. It also turns off any built-in cards you had turned off, which the import screen tells you about on its own line.

Can I change the cards?

Settings → Match rules, Throw effects or Penalties. Every card the app ships can be turned off and put back, and you can write your own.

What language does it speak?

English, Russian, German, Spanish, French, Czech, Italian and Polish. It opens in your phone's language if it knows it, and you can change it in Settings.

Does it collect anything about me?

No. See the privacy policy — the short version is that the app has no network connection at all.
