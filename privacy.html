Privacy Policy

Beerponger · last updated 14 August 2026

Beerponger does not collect any data.

The app has no accounts, no sign-up and no network connection. It makes no requests to any server, including mine. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app knows — your players, teams, matches, tournaments, cards and your chosen language — is written to files inside the app's own storage on your device. It never leaves the phone unless you choose to send it.

If you use Backup, the app writes those same contents to a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where that file goes is entirely your choice: whatever you send it to receives it, and I never see it.

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

Data shared with third parties

None. There are no third-party SDKs in the app.

Children

Beerponger is intended for adults and is rated 17+. It asks you to confirm you are 18 or over the first time it opens. That answer is stored on your device only.

Changes

If this policy ever changes — for example if a future version adds optional iCloud sync — this page will be updated before that version ships.
